# Scene 1: Metro platform (background plate)

**Note:** v1 was made with lantern lights and was locked before the lighting rule changed. For v2, replace "ornate iron lamp brackets" with "slim LED strip lights, neon tubes and recessed spotlights" and remove "warm amber lantern accents".

Locked result: `art/backgrounds/metro_platform/metro_platform_v1.png` (2752 x 1536).

```
Pre-rendered game background, wide 16:9, fixed camera at standing eye height looking down a gothic cyberpunk underground metro platform. Bold comic-book illustration with thick dark ink outlines and a painted, grimy texture: cracked stone, peeling plaster, wet tiled floor with splatter and stains. Hard-edged cel-shaded lighting with sharp shadow steps. Key colour deep magenta, secondary emerald green and teal, small warm amber lantern accents, near-black shadows, very high contrast.

Layout: a long empty tiled platform running from the lower foreground into the distance, wide and clear of objects so a character can walk on it. Arched gothic stone ceiling, ornate iron lamp brackets, gargoyle statues on pillars. Left foreground: a heavy stone pillar and a row of benches (to walk behind). Right side: the track bed and a dark tunnel mouth with magenta fog rolling out. Wet floor reflecting the neon glow. A tall open shaft of dark empty space above the platform, and large departure screens on the walls showing plain black glass with a thin glowing frame (screens and shaft left blank for an animation to be added later).

No people, no characters, no train, no falling code, no text, no title card, no border or frame, no UI, no logos. Single clean image, sharp focus, detailed.
```

Settings used: 16:9, style reference from the earlier metro concept, 4 variations, best floor chosen.
