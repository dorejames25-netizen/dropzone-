# Effect: glyph rain (animation layer)

Plays over the blank screens, the sign and the tall shaft in each scene. Black background so it can be added over the plate in the game.

```
Seamless looping animation, wide 16:9, pure black background. Vertical streams of falling glowing symbols, like digital rain, in thin columns at different speeds and lengths. The symbols are a mix of ancient Mayan-style glyphs, Celtic druid tree-alphabet marks, old hieroglyph-style carvings, simple emoji faces and numbers 0-9. Colours: emerald green and teal with occasional magenta and white-hot lead symbols at the head of each stream, soft glow, fading trails. Some columns slow and large, some fast and small, for depth. Dark gaps between columns. No scene, no objects, no text banner, no border, only the falling symbols on pure black.
```

If the generator does not loop cleanly, make one still of the symbol set on black and build the loop in Godot with a shader.
