```
PROJECT BRIEF (for mood and consistency, not to be drawn literally)

Nightmare Frequency is a cyberpunk-gothic noir adventure game set in a near future. Mankind has secretly been controlled for ages by a higher race called the Makers, who built humans as workers in the age of the Ancients. When humans launched satellites and built the first AI, bionic upgrades and virtual cyber centres, the Makers used the new network to release a data virus called the Nightmare Frequency. It warps virtual reality into reality, takes over bionic implants and minds, and in a single day took control of everything. Most of the public still does not know. A black-ops team of scientists, Project Nightmare Frequency, is racing to stop it, while many officials have already been taken.

History repeats: an earlier age ended in an extinction event, with the elite escaping into a virtual sleep and everyone else left behind. Time itself is a force called Aion, and things happened "aions ago".

Look and feel: gothic cathedral architecture inside a rain-slick neon city. Heavy shadow, silhouettes, wet reflective floors, thick comic-book ink outlines over painted, grimy texture. Key colour deep magenta with emerald green and teal, near-black shadows, very high contrast. Lighting is modern and futuristic, never old-fashioned: slim LED strip lights, neon tubes, recessed spotlights, flat light panels, caged industrial work lights and glowing holographic fixtures, in the room's own colours. Gothic is the architecture, not the lamps. No oil lanterns, gas lamps, candles or torches. Recurring motifs: glitching grinning faces inside screens and walls, and streams of falling glowing symbols made of ancient carved glyphs, emoji and numbers, as the virus bleeds into reality. The mood is eerie, beautiful and decaying, never cartoonish or silly.

Rules: original designs only, no real brands, logos, game or film characters, and no real-world religious symbols. Backgrounds are fixed-camera still scenes for a game, so keep floors clear for a character to walk on, and keep any people or creatures out of the background plates.
```
