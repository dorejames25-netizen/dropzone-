PROJECT BRIEF (for mood and consistency, not to be drawn literally)

Nightmare Frequency is a cyberpunk-gothic noir adventure game set in a near future. Mankind has secretly been controlled for ages by a higher race called the Makers, who built humans as workers in the age of the Ancients. When humans launched satellites and built the first AI, bionic upgrades and virtual cyber centres, the Makers used the new network to release a data virus called the Nightmare Frequency. It warps virtual reality into reality, takes over bionic implants and minds, and in a single day took control of everything. Most of the public still does not know. A black-ops team of scientists, Project Nightmare Frequency, is racing to stop it, while many officials have already been taken.

History repeats: an earlier age ended in an extinction event, with the elite escaping into a virtual sleep and everyone else left behind. Time itself is a force called Aion, and things happened "aions ago".

Look and feel: a future hybrid of gothic and cyberpunk. Gothic cathedral architecture (arches, ribbed vaults, stone columns, gargoyles) fitted out with modern technology inside a rain-slick neon city. Heavy shadow, silhouettes, wet reflective floors, thick comic-book ink outlines over painted, grimy texture. Hard-edged cel-shaded lighting with sharp shadow steps. Near-black shadows, very high contrast. One key colour per room. Lighting is modern and futuristic, never old-fashioned: slim LED strip lights, neon tubes, recessed spotlights, flat light panels, caged industrial work lights and glowing holographic fixtures, in the room's own colours. Gothic is the architecture, not the lamps. No oil lanterns, gas lamps, candles or torches. Recurring motifs: glitching grinning faces inside screens and walls, and streams of falling glowing symbols made of ancient carved glyphs, emoji and numbers, as the virus bleeds into reality. The mood is eerie, beautiful and decaying, never cartoonish or silly.

Rules for every image: original designs only, no real brands, logos, game or film characters, and no real-world religious symbols. These are fixed-camera still backgrounds for a game, so: wide 16:9, camera at standing eye height, a wide clear floor area a character can walk on with a visible edge, big foreground columns or arches to walk behind, a few blocked pieces of furniture that do not block the main path, at least two visible exits, and any screens, signs or tall shafts left as plain black glass with a thin glowing frame (blank for an animation added later). No people, no characters, no creatures, no falling code, no text, no title card, no border or frame, no UI, no logos. Single clean image, sharp focus, detailed. Same art style, line weight and level of detail as the reference image.
